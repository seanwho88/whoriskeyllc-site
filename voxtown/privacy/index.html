Privacy Policy

Voxtown — Last updated: 2026-08-25

Overview

Voxtown is a music-discovery app that shows you what people are listening to right now. We built it with a privacy-first mindset: listening data expires automatically and you control what you share. Voxtown does not request or store end-user device location.

This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. If you have questions, contact us at admin@whoriskeyllc.com.

Voxtown is operated by Whoriskey LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company, which is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. "We", "us", and "our" refer to Whoriskey LLC throughout.

1. What We Collect

Account & identity

  • Display name (chosen by you, optional)
  • Listener tag / persona (chosen by you, optional)
  • Account creation timestamp

Authentication tokens

  • Last.fm username (if you connect Last.fm), stored as a plain identifier

Listening activity

  • Song title, artist, and album for the track you are currently playing
  • Source of the track (Last.fm scrobble or manual entry)
  • Approximate timestamp when the track was posted
Listening records are automatically deleted after 7 days. We never store your full listening history.

Connections

  • Which users you have accepted a music-triggered connection request from (stored as a pair of anonymised user IDs)
  • Messages sent within a connection chat (stored only for the duration of the connection)

Badge progress

  • Aggregate counters only (e.g. "played 10 jazz tracks total"). We do not store the individual songs that contributed to a badge, and we do not store badge evidence sequences.

Device & diagnostics

  • None. Voxtown ships no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect screen views, feature interactions, device identifiers, or crash data.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Last.fm password
  • Device location of any kind (GPS, network, or geohash)
  • Listening history beyond the 7-day rolling window
  • The individual songs used to unlock a badge (only the final counter is stored)
  • Your real name, phone number, or email address (unless you contact us directly)
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Browser cookies or cross-site tracking identifiers

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Live feed: Your current track (if shared) is displayed to other Voxtown users.
  • Badge system: Aggregate counters are used to award story badges and surface personalised milestones.
  • Connections: Connection requests are triggered by shared listening moments and routed between the two users involved.
  • Business map: Registered venues and their current track may be displayed on a map view. Venues enter their own address; we do not use your device location.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your listening data for advertising profiling.

4. Third-Party Services

Last.fm / AudioScrobbler

If you connect a Last.fm account, we periodically query the Last.fm API for your recent scrobbles. Voxtown was granted conditional commercial-use approval for the Last.fm API on 2026-07-29, subject to rate and volume limits we enforce in our own code. See Last.fm's Privacy Policy.

Firebase / Google

We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in only. We do not use Firestore or any other Firebase database; your data is stored in our own PostgreSQL database. You can sign in with Google, in which case Google may share your email address and display name with us. Google may process data in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

Apple (Sign in with Apple)

You can also sign in with Apple. When you do, Apple may share your name and either your real email address or a private relay email (your choice at sign-in time) with us. We treat the relay address the same as a normal email and never attempt to deanonymise it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Listening activity: automatically purged after 7 days via server-side TTL.
  • Connection messages: deleted when a connection is removed by either party.
  • Account data: retained until you delete your account.
  • Badge counters: retained as long as your account is active; deleted on account deletion.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and all associated data (in-app: Settings → Delete Account)
  • Disconnect Last.fm, which revokes our access to future data from those services
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing admin@whoriskeyllc.com

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

7. Security

Authentication tokens are stored in platform-native secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). Data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Our backend enforces row-level security so users can only access their own records. We apply the principle of least privilege to all internal service accounts.

8. Children

Voxtown is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
